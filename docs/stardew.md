# Music of the Valley: How the Soundtrack of Stardew Valley Impacts Storytelling

## About the Music

The Stardew Valley Official Soundtrack is composed by ConcernedApe, the game's creator.

The original soundtrack featured 70 songs. Since then, the following have been added with each update:

- Four songs from v1.1 in November 2017
- Three songs from v1.3 in August 2018
- Fourteen songs from v1.4 in November 2019
- Nine songs from v1.5 in December 2020
